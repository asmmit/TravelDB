About TravelDB Decision Support System

TravelDB is an intelligent, fuel-aware travel decision support system built specifically for the Chennai – Vellore – Bengaluru highway corridor (NH 48 / AH 45) in South India.

Designed as an advanced Interdisciplinary Design Project (IDP) and Database Management Systems (DBMS) thesis, the platform bridges relational ACID guarantees with high-speed graph routing to solve the classic Gas Station Problem with Non-Uniform Pricing and Safety Reserves.

MySQL 8.0 System of Record (BCNF & SRID 4326)
C-Core In-Memory Routing (sub-10ms Dijkstra)
MoRTH High-Fatality Blackspot Avoidance
Real-time Open-Meteo Weather Drag Factors

Terms of Service & Navigation Disclaimers

1. Advisory Purpose Only

All routes, fuel consumption estimations, and arrival predictions computed by TravelDB are generated using mathematical models, ARAI standard mileage figures, and historical road gradients. Actual highway fuel consumption may vary depending on driver behavior, vehicle cargo, air conditioning usage, and live traffic congestion. TravelDB is an advisory decision support system; drivers must obey official Indian road signs, speed limits, and traffic police directives at all times.

2. MoRTH Accident Blackspot Data Attribution

Highway accident blackspots and fatality statistics displayed in this application are derived from official public reports published by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Government of India. High-fatality zones are indicated to help drivers exercise extra vigilance and do not imply that any road authority has endorsed or audited this tool.

3. Fuel Pricing & Station Availability

Retail fuel pricing data is based on official Indian Oil Corporation Limited (IOCL) benchmark district rates for Tamil Nadu and Karnataka. Operating hours, pump amenities, and dispensing availability at individual fuel stations are subject to independent vendor operations.

Privacy Policy & Emergency Contact Protocol

Your user profile information (including full name, age, phone number, and emergency contact phone number) is stored securely in the local MySQL database. In accordance with safety best practices:

  • Emergency Contact Usage: Your designated emergency contact number is retained exclusively for trip assistance, roadside SOS generation, and vehicle telemetry audit records.
  • Zero Telemetry Selling: TravelDB does not transmit, sell, or disclose your simulated GPS telemetry, route histories, or personal data to third-party advertisers.
  • Local Data Sovereignty: All geospatial indices, R-tree queries, and relational tables execute directly on your private server instance.
