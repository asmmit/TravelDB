# TravelDB Data Source Register

| Source Name | Provider & Official URL | License / Terms | Update Frequency | Rate Limits | Storage Allowed? | TravelDB Purpose | Known Gaps & Mitigations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **OpenStreetMap Road Network** | OpenStreetMap Foundation (`openstreetmap.org`) via OSMnx / Geofabrik | **ODbL 1.0** (Open Database License) | Updated continuously by community | Geofabrik: none. Overpass: ~2 concurrent requests, max 10k calls/day. | **YES**. Unrestricted local persistence in MySQL. Requires UI attribution: *"© OpenStreetMap contributors"*. | Primary road graph topology (`ROAD_NODE`, `ROAD_EDGE`). | Rural speed limits and turn restrictions may be unmapped. Mitigated by `default_speed_kph` mapped by `road_type` and flagged `speed_source='default'`. |
| **OpenStreetMap POIs** | OSM Overpass API (`overpass-api.de`) | **ODbL 1.0** | Continuous | Overpass rate limits (slot queue) | **YES**. Stored in MySQL `FUEL_STATION` and `POI`. | Gas stations, hospitals, police, EV charging, rest areas. | Opening hours and operating status are crowd-reported; not all pumps have brand or fuel type. Mitigated by `verified_status` and crowdsourced user reports. |
| **TomTom Traffic Flow** | TomTom Developer Portal (`api.tomtom.com`) | TomTom Developer Terms | Real-time (refreshed every 1-3 min) | 2,500 free calls/day, 5 QPS | **TRANSIENT ONLY**. Storing to build historical clearinghouses or **train ML/AI models is strictly forbidden**. | Dynamic edge cost adjustment (`effective_speed`) on key corridor sample points. | Does not cover minor village/tertiary roads. Mitigated by falling back to static edge speed limits with a visible banner. |
| **TomTom Incidents** | TomTom Developer Portal (`api.tomtom.com`) | TomTom Developer Terms | Real-time | 2,500 free calls/day | **TRANSIENT ONLY**. | Closed roads and delay warnings mapped to `INCIDENT` and `INCIDENT_EDGE`. | Incident clearance lag (reported incident may already have cleared). Mitigated by `is_active` expiry and crowdsourced confirmations. |
| **Open-Meteo Weather** | Open-Meteo (`open-meteo.com`) | **CC-BY 4.0** | Hourly updates / 15-min forecasts | 10,000 calls/day, 5,000/hr, 600/min | **YES**. Historical storage, caching, and model training permitted with attribution. | Rain, wind, visibility penalties applied to road friction and fuel consumption. | Grid resolution is ~1-11 km; localized flash flooding may not be reflected. Mitigated by crowdsourced road hazard reports. |
| **Historical Accidents & Blackspots** | MoRTH Road Accidents in India / data.gov.in / TN Police OGD | Government Open Data License (GODL India) | Annual publications | Batch download | **YES**. Stored permanently in `ACCIDENT_HISTORICAL` and `BLACKSPOT`. | Baseline risk weighting for road edges based on district accident density. | Historical data is aggregated at district/highway level, not per edge. Loaded honestly at district granularity. |
| **Crowdsourced User Reports** | TravelDB Users (`USER_REPORT` table) | Proprietary / Internal | Real-time as reported | Rate limited per user IP/account | **YES**. Internal system of record. | Potholes, waterlogging, unexpected closures, station out-of-fuel. | Trustworthiness varies. Mitigated by upvotes/downvotes, reporter trust score, and auto-expiry. |
